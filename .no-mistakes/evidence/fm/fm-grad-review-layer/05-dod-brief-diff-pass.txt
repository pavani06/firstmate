- --claim change --note "{your one-line summary}"` (use `--claim no-change` when you deliberately changed 
nothing). Fetch the remote-tracking ref itself with that explicit refspec and diff against 
`origin/<default-branch>`, never the local one: pooled project clones do not keep their local default branch 
current, a bare single-branch fetch can refresh only `FETCH_HEAD` and leave `origin/<default-branch>` stale,
